Brand colors
------------
Primary background: #0A0A0A
Primary gold: #C6A75E
White option: #FFFFFF
Black option: #000000

Recommended usage
-----------------
- Use *_transparent_gold.png on black, charcoal, or dark photography.
- Use *_transparent_black.png on white or very light backgrounds.
- Use *_transparent_white.png on dark photos when the gold version lacks enough contrast.
- Keep generous clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, add gradients, or change proportions.
